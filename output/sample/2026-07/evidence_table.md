**Reading this table.** `HVFHV pickups` and `Rate per 100k` use **all zone-attributable pickups** (flagged rides included); `P90 request-to-pickup` is computed on the **KPI population** (in_zone_population), a slightly smaller set. Different denominators, on purpose: the rate needs every ride a complaint could refer to, the P90 needs rides with a defensible wait.

**Numerator undercount.** 11 passenger/company complaints could not be mapped to a zone and are therefore absent from the numerator, so the published rate understates the true one by about 4.4% (if unmapped complaints resemble mapped ones). They are counted, never assigned by guess.

311 complaints mapped to a zone: 1,342; no coordinates: 29; outside all zones: 2; passenger/company complaints mapped: 237/248. 311 records are contextual customer-friction signals at borough/zone-month grain, **not** ride-level interactions.

## Known / Unknown / Assumption / Limitation

**Known**
- 10,000 completed HVFHV rides; 9,869 pass the lifecycle rules (KPI population).
- on_scene_datetime is populated for 100.00% of rides but independently observed and ordered for 93.22%.
- Rides with pickup before request exist; they are flagged, not deleted.
- 1,342 FHV complaints to 311 map to a taxi zone; most FHV complaints come from non-passengers (street safety).

**Unknown**
- Which rides were pre-scheduled (no flag): request_datetime may be a booked slot for some rides.
- Unserved or cancelled requests: only completed trips are published.
- Which ride a 311 complaint refers to: 311 has no ride ID and is never linked to rides.
- Proprietary interventions (dispatch rules, incentives, surge): not observable in public data.

**Assumptions**
- HVFHV timestamps are NYC local time; weather is joined on the request hour (America/New_York).
- One weather grid cell (40.808434, -74.0199) represents the whole city.
- 311 state-plane coordinates and the TLC zone shapefile share EPSG:2263 (verified by borough agreement).
- ride_id = row ordinal in the checksummed raw file; thresholds are in validation_report.json -> thresholds.

**Limitations**
- Request-to-pickup time is the rider-experienced interval, not driver wait time.
- KPI 3 is a lower bound: rides whose on_scene equals pickup to the second (no curb wait, so a 100% driver-arrival share) are not in its population.
- KPI 5 is a lower bound too: passenger complaints that did not map to a zone are absent from its numerator.
- Completed trips only (survivorship). Zone, borough and weather results are associations, not causes.
- 311 is sparse, covers all FHV types and reflects the propensity to complain, hence borough grain.
- No intervention data: the effect of operational actions cannot be evaluated.
